When you can return

You can return a perfume within 3 days of receiving it, but only if the package is unopened and the seal is not broken.

When we cannot accept a return

If the package has been opened or the seal has been broken, we cannot accept a return. Please be sure of your choice before you open your perfume.

How to request a return

Contact us on WhatsApp within 3 days of receiving your order, with your name and order details. We will tell you the next steps.

When you place an order, we use the name, phone number and delivery address you give us. If you create an account, we also store your email, your saved delivery details and your wishlist.

Only to process and deliver your orders, to contact you about them, and to fill in your details at checkout. We do not sell your personal information.

Account details are stored securely with Google Firebase. Orders are sent to us through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy terms.
